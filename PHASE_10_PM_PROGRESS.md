## 🎯 PROGRESS METRICS

### **Overall Progress Metrics**
- **Phase Completion**: Percentage of phases completed
- **Task Completion**: Percentage of tasks completed
- **Milestone Completion**: Percentage of milestones achieved
- **On-Time Delivery**: Percentage of milestones delivered on time

### **Quality Metrics**
- **Bug Count**: Number of bugs by severity
- **Test Pass Rate**: Percentage of tests passing
- **Code Coverage**: Percentage of code covered by tests
- **Performance Compliance**: Percentage of performance criteria met

### **Resource Metrics**
- **Resource Utilization**: Percentage of allocated resources used
- **Budget Utilization**: Percentage of budget spent
- **Personnel Availability**: Percentage of planned time available

- **Tool**: Jira
- **Fields**: Task ID, Title, Status, Assignee, Due Date, Progress

- **Update Frequency**: Weekly
- **Visibility**: Project Lead + Stakeholders

### **Budget Tracking**
- **Tool**: Spreadsheet or finance system
- **Fields**: Category, Budgeted, Spent, Remaining, Variance
- **Update Frequency**: Weekly
- **Visibility**: Project Lead + Finance

## 🎯 REPORTING CADENCE

### **Daily Standup**
- **Duration**: 15 minutes
- **Participants**: All team members
- **Format**: What did you do yesterday? What will you do today? Any blockers?
- **Output**: Daily standup notes

### **Weekly Status Report**
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Participants**: All team members + stakeholders
- **Format**: Phase progress, task progress, risks, issues
- **Output**: Weekly status report

### **Phase Review**
- **Duration**: 2 hours
- **Participants**: All team members
- **Format**: Phase completion review, lessons learned, next phase kickoff
- **Output**: Phase review document

### **Stakeholder Update**
- **Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Participants**: Stakeholders only
- **Format**: High-level progress, risks, timeline
- **Output**: Stakeholder update summary

## 🎯 ESCALATION PROCEDURES

### **Level 1: Team Lead**
- **Trigger**: Task blocked for >1 day
- **Action**: Team Lead resolves or escalates
- **Timeline**: Resolve within 2 days

### **Level 2: Project Lead**
- **Trigger**: Task blocked for >2 days or phase delayed
- **Action**: Project Lead resolves or escalates
- **Timeline**: Resolve within 3 days

### **Level 3: Steering Committee**
- **Trigger**: Phase delayed for >1 week or budget overrun
- **Action**: Steering Committee decides
- **Timeline**: Resolve within 5 days

### **Level 4: Executive Sponsor**
- **Trigger**: Project delayed for >2 weeks or critical issue
- **Action**: Executive Sponsor decides
- **Timeline**: Resolve within 7 days
